Azeroth: A Moment in Time

A Moment in Time Is a long ongoing Role Play/Dungeons & Dragons/Story Writing venture that holds its roots well over 25 years ago with a close group of real-life friends, family, and our shared love for writing and fantasy world-building that we all grew up with.

Starting with our love for D&D and moving onto worlds such as Warcraft, this crazy venture helped fill in the void in some of our lives and gave us a creative outlet to express ourselves through the worlds and stories we would come up with.

Even though today our lives as we've grown have driven us physically apart, we do our best to stay in contact with each other and continue to take joy in the hobbies we still love.

So Welcome, come step into our Inn, pull up a chair, and enjoy some of our concepts, characters, and adventures!

Some tips on how the site works

We are, incredible lore nuts when it comes to the world and characters that inhabit it. Whenever possible we like to offer reference sources, rather to on site or off site locations when speaking of places or details.

Whenever we need to discuss a point in time, we follow Warcraft's reference of BDP (Before the Dark Portal) and ADP (After the Dark Portal).
Year Zero (ADP 0) is the start of the First War when the Dark Portal was originally opened. World of Warcraft started in 25 ADP. It is used as chronological reference following the established Timeline with links to the wiki for further curious minds to enjoy.

You may also notice the use of mouse over tool-tips (note they may not display on mobile). These are in place to help show the source of a link when we wish to refrence something, for example: Pandaria's Hystory. Or say a point to a Timeline reference like (40 ADP).
You may also find non link references aimed to offer a bit more context. All to help the flow of the site.

A NOTE ON OUR LORE & COMMUNITY

We strive to respect the established lore of these worlds, including the distinct cultures, biology, lifespans, and traditions of the fantasy peoples in our stories. These differences shape our characters relationships, choices, and ambitions. We prefer to explore those differences rather than treat every species as a real-life human with a different appearance.

This notice is in place following a serious incident involving targeted harassment and false accusations against members of our group, after outside individuals own assumptions on characters they neither owned nor created took an extreme turn that became a nightmare we wish not to have repeated. Thus we have established a formal statement explaining our creative principles, the importance of this distinction, and our expectations regarding respectful conduct.

We strongly encourage visitors to read our character biographies and supporting lore before making assumptions about the characters or their creators.

Please Read of Our Lore & Community Statement for our creative approach, examples from Warcraft lore, and the reason we established these community boundaries. Yes we will disclose what happened for this to be necessary in hopes no one else has to endure what our member faced.
