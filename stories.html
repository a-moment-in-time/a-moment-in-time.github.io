Logs & Stories

Welcome to the main archive hosting all our written ventures. Here we will be gradually releasing our D&D sessions as they are converted into more story driven format based on our logs and session notes. For more incite on how that processes is undertaken, you can view how we plan everything.

This is WIP as we are still migrating over from World Anvil, and testing website designs that work best, including a system to publish our stories easily from text files and have it render properly as with the demo below. We may also offer mirrors to our stories over on Ao3 and Ellipsus, but we haven't settled on anything on that yet.

Last please keep in mind, we do follow closely with the Warcraft Lore. We do not turn all our characters into 'real-life humans with different skin tones' It's stupid we must declare this, yes, but some people seem to insist things are more to real life then accepting how things may work in a fictional setting.

Rating Color Guide

Story header colors indicate the audience rating at a glance:

  • General AudiencesSuitable for a broad audience.
  • TeenStronger emotions, peril, or mild mature themes.
  • MatureAdult themes or more intense material / serious violence.
  • ExplicitMay include graphic violence or explicit sexual material.
  • Not Rated / PendingNo rating yet, or the story's direction is still undecided.

Note: Color is a guide, not a content warning. Please be sure to check each story's description and warnings before reading.
