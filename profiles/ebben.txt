[HISTORY] <h3>Ripped Away</h3>
Due to events that would come to pass in other timeways, events around this one had unfortunately drawn the attention of the Infinite Dragonflight. In their desire to use him in their plans, they had begun infiltrating differing timelines and after several failures managed to finally snatch a single egg from its world and brought it to their own timeline resembling an ‘End Time’. With the egg in their clutches, their plans were set in motion: to twist and corrupt the whelp into another Deathwing to use as their own tool against the timeways for their own goals. However, knowing the events from other timelines they didn't want the risk of what may happen if allowed to hatch on its own; given the power they had shown to have even fresh from the egg; they had ripped the whelp from the egg prematurely to begin their task of break him in mind and body.

He was passed off to a captive Red Dragon whom had been used as a forced breeder to help ‘keep him alive’ with her life magic during his breaking.  Threatening to do her harm if he were to die given the history between the flights. While the Infinite could heal, their methods revolved around reversing time, making events and wounds fade as they never happened...but that wouldn't work when trying to break a young mind. Yet despite being of the Black Flight the Red only saw a whelp in dire need. As weak and pained as he was she offered him the option to be allowed to pass on, as she was unafraid of what they would do to herself as they have already done their worst. For a body so frail the whelp showed a strong will to live, and so she did what she could for him, promising that she would see him free so long as that desire to live remained. She names the whelp <b>Ebbyion</b> for the time, and cares for him like a mother would her own.
<h3>Mending</h3>
After around a month in her care, tending to him every time he was taken to be 'worked on', she had helped slip him off to other’s who opposed the Infinite and were able to smuggle the tiny whelp out. He was passed on to a red, Saulstrasz who took him the rest of the way to another he had also helped rescue from the Infinites clutches years prior: <a href="?char=sandria" onclick="switchProfile(event, 'sandria')">Sandria</a>, a red/bronze hybrid created by the Infinite. Though known by those in the small settlement she hid in as Maetel Plesner, named after the human paladin Callahan Plesner who had agreed to help conceal her as his own daughter.

The little whelp was presented as ‘another example of the Infinite Dragonflights increasing cruelty’, the whelps left wing was shattered, left arm nearly was torn off and it was hard to tell where the bruises ended and his scale color began. Being told what had happened to the little whelp, Mae took it upon herself to look over and care for him, taking on the role of a big sister. When she asked what he was called, Saulstrasz explained they didn’t know what name he would have taken and that they would just have to wait for the little one to gain strength to tell them. For the time being, the captive red, Mae’s own mother, had named the little one Ebbyion.

It took nearly a month before Ebbyion was strong enough to take on a mortal form aided by the suppression enchantment of the pendant he wore that helped in concealing himself, and a few days more before he started speaking for the first time, though he was still too weak to move around on his own. When Mae finally asked him for his name, his true name he would have called himself if he hadn’t been stolen from his time, he merely stated they already knew it. He was Ebbyion. With all the care and aid they had shown and given him that was the name he chose to keep. Rather he knew what name he would have gone by, he never let onto. Callahan then offered the boy the mortal name Ebben Plesner, sealing his new family ties that he had accepted and cherished.

But as he grew stronger it became clear the boy had issues, both physically and mentally.

While his left wing could not be mended, his left arm was able to be saved. But the limb was weak and its joints lacked in range of motion unable to properly lift it above his head at the shoulder, and elbow not able to fully expand or contract (Stuck between 45digrees to 130digrees on good days). His left hand also displayed weakness in grip and possible nerve damage. The damage to his mangled and disjoined wing manifested on his mortal form as a deformation to his left shoulder blade and led to him suffering chromic pain in the joint. On bad days the muscles would spasm leaving him crippled in agony, and giving him flashes of the torture he endured.

Maetel tried to help him with this by teaching him swordplay to build up strength and helping to stretch the limb to hopefully avoid it becoming stiff or losing any further range of mobility. Callahan also aided in a much different way, by encouraging and teaching Ebben how to play the violin. The use of his arms to handle the musical instrument; though difficult at first; became beneficial to retaining the use of his arm and helping the dexterity of his hand. This daily routine did help alleviate most of the spasm's he suffered from and made the regular pain he felt in the joints tolerable.

His mind however was another matter. The trauma he had suffered from the Infinite had left its mark, he was fully aware inside the egg and understood what was happening to him throughout the ordeal: His egg being stolen, being ripped out of it before his time, and of their plans to try and twist him into becoming another Deathwing. Such a thought along with the torment they had inflicted upon him had left the boy terrified. He also swore he could hear whispers, and had dark nightmares that warped the world around him sending him into panic attacks and fits of terror. His only comfort being his sister who helped ground him in reality no matter how dark the whispers or twisted the visions. She was his strength, his shield, his wings.

To try and help him combat his fears Maetel had encouraged him and started teaching him how to push past the pendant's suppression like she could to allow their Dragon Magic to manifest. Something she herself was discouraged in doing as it could expose herself, their powers and natures are kept hidden for a reason. It was difficult at first with his fears so deeply rooted, but gradually his power started to come forth. Being a Black Dragon granted Ebben domain over the earth able to shape and manipulate it at will. That as they found combined with Maetel's magic over both Life and Time due to her hybrid nature became an ideal combination.

However, as more of his power came forward and grew in strength... thing's backfired horribly. The pendants were not designed with Black dragons in mind as they no longer existed when they were created, let alone had it been thought that one could push through the enchantment.
<h3>The Downfall</h3>
On one quiet night, while Ebbyion was working his draconic earth magic to shape the land around him, he pushed it forward too hard, and his earth magic had caused his enchanted amulet; the very thing binding his form and hiding him from the Infinite; to crack.

All at once, his full power came rushing forward as the enchantment was disabled, causing a outward surge of magic he couldn't contain. This power, his essence of his true self became like a beacon being the only black dragon in this End Time. He rushed to Mae with his cracked pendant apologizing for what he had done, his fear mounting he felt he couldn't stay and just as quickly he bolted while Mae tried to undo the damage to his pendant. It wasn’t long before the Infinite had found him despite his efforts to flee and tried to steal him back so he could resume his role for them. They taunted him, motioning to the world around them and the corrupted earth elementals that were roaming and blamed HIM for it, claimed that was what his power could do... He remained frozen, fixated, terrified and horrified recalling what they wanted him to become... this power... he feared it. 

Maetel would find them looming over Ebben's form, taunting the boy and did the only thing she could think of to save him: she ripped her own pendant off her neck allowing her own power to come forward, exposing and drawing the Infinite to herself instead.

For the first time, her full power was at her command and she was able to use the power of her bronze side to pause time around them and counter the Infinites own time magic. Grabbing Ebbyion she threw her own pendant around his neck as they fled, hiding him away as she continued without him while trying to time warp his pendant back before it had been cracked.

She eventually returned, tired and battered, and with Ebbyion's mended pendant around her neck. But this event had only solidified Ebbyion’s own fears. He refused to touch his own power ever again, terrified of what that power could turn him into, what he could end up becoming… Being reminded of the corrupted nature of his flight even if he himself was supposedly 'free' of it, and what the Infinite desired of him haunted his mind.

That faithful event however led to the sibling's downfall... they were now known, and the Infinite was after them forcing a desperate plan. Saulstrasz whisked the small family away to the Nexus, an ancient ice fortress found in the center of Coldarra just off of Borean Tundra that use to be home to the Blue Dragonflight before the timeline had been twisted into this darker shadow. There, along with a few bronze that resisted the Infinite's corruption, they hoped the magic of the Nexus would help mask them long enough to set a plan into motion: to escape this timeway into another.

But not all plans work out as one would hope.

The Infinite found them just before the portal was opened. Saulstrasz gave his life, his neck ripped open before their stunned eyes to try and delay the Infinite from getting close. Pulling the brother and sister from their shock, Callahan dragged them to the portal as it opened, and shoved them through… Maetel and Ebben would land on the other side, and watch in horror as the portal closed… trapping them alone together in an unknown time-way somewhere in the past.

(arriving somewhere near the start of the Wrath of the Lich King expansion before Yogg-Saron's defeat)

[NOTES]
<b>KEY DETAIL: </b> He wears a gemmed amulet that holds a powerful enchantment that suppresses his Dragon side, binding him to a Mortal Form and giving him the full aura of a Mortal, masking his true nature from all even from other Dragons.

Ebben is an AU Wrathion, always has been. He started as a thought from another friend Jordan, with the idea of "What if the Infinite had captured him from the timeline?" that ended up evolving into "What if the Infinite stole his egg from another timeline with the plans to twist that version of him into another Deathwing to use as a weapon against the time-ways?"

Ebbyion is an AU (Alternate Universe) Wrathion. This fact however is largely unknown by all but perhaps himself, though he hasn't given any signs of being aware.

The only hint of him being an AU Wrathion would be his voice as it is identical.

Ebbyion is OLDER than Wrathion, due to the years he lived in 'End Time', plus arriving to Azeroth's past during events of Wrath of the Lich King, nearly TWO YEARS before Wrathion's egg would be laid.

Due to Ebbyion's upbringing and what happened to him he has a lot of mental scaring and is very shy, timid and withdrawn. His (adopted) sister <a href="?char=sandria" onclick="switchProfile(event, 'sandria')">Sandria's</a> over-protectiveness of him hadn't helped in that regard. It takes some work for him to open up and be more himself. He does have his 'Wrathion' moments, however.

Although dragons have full control over their Mortal Forms; changing the appearance at will; Ebbyion hasn't really changed the appearance of his own Mortal Form much and continues to look almost the same as when he first gained it. This is mainly due to him not having any real need to change it.

Ebben has taken on the mortal guise of an Orc as an alternative to his normal human form. This form is rarely used and only came into play when he and his sister needed to cross Horde territories. It's also a homage to an Orc Shaman he and his sister befriended early on who had figured out what the siblings really were.

Outside when he first hatched, he has never taken on his true form.

Ebbyion does have some mild body dysmorphia and doesn't like being 'exposed'. Not helped that the scars and marks on his body always manifest on his Mortal Form. A step in the right direction has been getting him relaxed in shorter sleeved tops, allowing at least his forearms to be visible.

His left arm is somewhat disabled. He can't lift it above his head at the shoulder, and the elbow has a limited range of motion, between 45, and 130 degrees due to damage he suffered as a whelp that nearly ended with his arm torn off.
A hint to when things are becoming too much for him is he'll reach around and grip tight his left arm, and may try to angle his body so his left side isn't as exposed.

He suffers from chronic pain in his left shoulder (where the scar on his arm leads to) that he has dealt with all his life and has become so use to it that its 'normal'. This pain is due to his mangled and disjoined wing; an injury that was inflicted along with his torn arm; that never mended properly and manifests as a deformation on his shoulder, it is also why he cant lift the arm above his head.

Ebben was meant to be twisted, both mind and body into another Deathwing, and such a process had started before he was rescued. He is fully aware of this and the ordeal has left its mental scars. The idea is terrifying to him and has made him fearful of HIMSELF and the power he holds and what he feels it will turn him into.

Elements of his appearance is referenced from an artist I like who has an adorable style in how they draw Wrathion; mainly his hair style and eyes; before anyone knew what his new model would look like (artist poofed during legion). Like with long hair and ponytail.. I did plan for him to grow his hair out like that at some point as a throw back to his original design, back when every character I made came with long hair.
