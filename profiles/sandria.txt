[INFORMATION]
Sandria, or Maetel as she is known mostly as, is a young adult Drake. But to the majority she appears to be an average human, with nothing really sticking out about her although something may feel ‘off’ about her to any strong magic users if around her for too long.  She dons simple plate armor of silver and bronze coloration's along with a Dragonbone Great-sword strapped to her back. Or just simple leathers when in more casual or work wear. There are times when she may forgo her two handed sword for that of a one hander and shield when shes required to do more than just swing a sword.  

Under her mortal visage she had trained as a Paladin, though only hint of her paladin status being an old but well kept Libram she often has tied to her belt. Although rarely seen as she often keeps it tucked under her shirt for safe keeping, she also wears a purple gemed gold amulet bound by a golden chain around her neck, this amulet being the source of what makes her feel so ‘off’ as its enchanted to block her dragon side from being detected along with suppressing that side of her.  

In her true drake form, any direct connection to either the bronze or red dragonflight may be hard to place even for other dragons, although with out her amulet its clear to other dragon kind of her duel nature.  Her body shape and tone is average for any young adult drake though her wing muscles are slightly under formed due to lack of use as she don’t take her true form often. 


[HISTORY] <H3>The Dark Times</h3>It was in a time twisted world resembling ‘end time’, a world created by the Infinite Dragonflight that Sandria’s egg was laid in the lands of Northrend, but her and her clutch mates would not be normal. The true reasoning behind the time twisted experiments were closely guarded but for whatever reason the Infinites began with experimenting with dragon eggs using knowledge from the past, aiming to create a hybrid between their own and that of the Red Flight. Power of life and time in one. 

Although such a goal was not so straightforward, for even if the two flights did interbreed the eggs would follow the sire's side. A true hybrid could only be created, and so these eggs had to be… altered after they had been lad by the red female they had under their control. Following in the path of experiments that originally created the Chromatic Dragonflight.

There was word of several failed clutches, past attempts that didn’t create the desired results, eggs that failed to take to the experiments if they survived the twisting time warps done to them at all. But out of this one clutch of seven eggs, three were looking promising, showing signs of partial if not full hybridization. 

But before hatching, these three eggs appeared to have just… disappeared. Even time-warping back to see what had become of them didn’t bring about any results, as if blocked… or erased. Curse them remaining bronze! With how many times the experiment had failed and how much went into warping the eggs while keeping the young inside alive they couldn’t afford to lose their first and only possible success, plus new eggs were becoming on short supply.

Sandria was among those three, and only one of two that survived hatching. An enchanted amulet designed to both suppress and mask their true selves as well as give them the aura of a mortal had been draped over her egg to hide it from the Infinite, and was slipped around her neck as soon as she broke free from the shell with assistance.

With the aid of one of the few still pure bronze, her form was time-warped to age her up just enough to not draw attention, but enough for her to enter her drake stage and gain her mortal form as soon as possible. A simple form at first, but a form that she would be told to remain in from then on less she be found and used for whatever Murozond and his flight had planned for her.

She had been put under the care of Saulstrasz, a male red dragon in the guise of a night elf. He would become a makeshift father figure to her after she learned he had a connection to her mother whom still remained a captive to be force bred by the Infinite. But it was too risky for him to always remain in her life. Dragon’s in this timeway only remained intact due to staying apart and in hiding. Something that very much went against their communal nature.

<H3>New Ties</h3> To keep her safe from the Infinites plans she had been passed off again, this time to a Human Paladin: Callahan Plesner, who had agreed to tend to the young girl. He gave her the mortal name <b>Maetel Plesner</b>, and took up the role of father to her in the mortal world, though she would always see Saul as her ‘true father’ even though she knew he was not really her sire. Saulstrasz would continue to return from time to time to bring things she may need and be sure she was safe, but he didn't dare stay with her for longer than necessary for her own safety.

Although Maetel appeared to be thriving in her new life; and kept busy thanks to Callahan training her as a blacksmith; seeing Callahan also making his rounds, healing others and aiding them, it was becoming frustrating for her. As time passed, knowing, feeling that she had this power developing within but no means to use it… knowing she could be helping people much like her mortal father was doing, and perhaps even more than that. It was like an itch she couldn’t scratch, but the spark was there, and she began to push against her binds.

Much to the displeasure of Saulstrasz and concerns of Callahan, traces of her Draconic magic started to manifest at her command. It was a shock that she had somehow managed to overcome the pendant's magic to push her own forward, and they warned that it put her and everyone there at great risk. Their powers are kept at bay for a reason as it broke the illusion they were trying to keep up and the Infinite Dragonflight; if they caught trace of it; could use her own power to find her. 

There was also the risk that if the other mortals there knew what she was, they may be tempted to give her up out of fear of being found harboring her or in hopes of gaining some kind of bargain. Due to this, and Mae’s strong desire to do more for people, Callahan had agreed to train her as a Paladin, as well in the ways of the holy light.

Though a dragon has never been herd of wielding power other then their native magical abilities, if anything training as a Paladin would hopefully give Maetel a discipline, another means to keep her mind focused and a belief to follow like he did. While it did help in giving her another outlet for herself, perhaps through her growing desire to help, the struggled of their lives, and maybe her own stubbornness and newfound prayers... the Light answered her, and she embraced it.

A dragon wielding the holy light was unheard of, but it came to her as nearly as easily as her own native magics. Rather than question it, they reinforced it. Callahan further taught her how to properly weird it and at last she could openly start to use it to help those around her. Though it didn't fully halt her use of her draconic abilities... after all to her they also had their uses, they were apart of her and that itch to use her innate abilities was still there.

<H3>A new life saved</h3>Then, on one faithful evening, Saulstrasz arrived with an unexpected horror that he described as ‘another example of the Infinite Dragonflights increasing cruelty’. A small black whelp. His left wing was shattered, left arm nearly was torn off and it was hard to tell where the bruises ended and his scale color began. How could the little one still be alive? 

Mae was told the little one had been stolen from his own timeline, ripped from the egg before he was ready, and tormented in the plans to warp him into another Deathwing. He was only saved thanks to Mae’s own mother who had been given the whelp to keep him alive through his suffering... but had managed to pass him off to be smuggled out to safety.

Maetel didn’t think twice about taking in the premature whelp under her care and quickly adopted the boy as a brother. Though the name he would have taken had been lost to time he had been given the name <a href="?char=ebben" onclick="switchProfile(event, 'ebben')">Ebbyion</a> by Mae’s mother, a name the boy accepted as his own even when asked what name he would have gone by. 

Callahan than gave the boy the mortal name <b>Ebben Plesner</b>, sealing their family ties. The boy’s recovery was slow, and it was clear there would be lasting traumas, both mentally and physically, but Mae was always by his side to help him through it, as she would say: she would be his strength, his shield, his wings.

<H3>A blip in time</h3>As the Infinite Dragonflight grew increasingly active, the few remaining Bronze dragons who had resisted their corruption began searching for a way to escape the twisted world they had come to inhabit. Their efforts eventually led to a daring plan: to open passages into other timelines, sending those willing to leave into varying points of an altered past, where the terrible events that had brought about their own world's downfall had never occurred. It was a dangerous undertaking, but one that offered something their dying world could no longer promise. A future.

Among those selected for the journey were Sandria and her family. When the time came to open their passage, Sandria volunteered to go through first, intending to use her own command over time to help stabilize the portal from the other side so the others could safely follow.

Unfortunately, things did not go as planned.

Upon arriving in the unfamiliar past, Sandria was discovered by a Bronze dragon who mistook her unusual magic for that of the Infinite Dragonflight. Before she could properly explain herself, the Bronze severed the connection, closing the portal and leaving her stranded in a time that was not her own. Despite their efforts, those left behind were unable to reestablish the passage, and Sandria was left with no way of returning, and no way to know of their fates.

With no means of knowing what had become of her family, Sandria was forced to make a life for herself in this unfamiliar world. Years passed as she learned to navigate her new surroundings, growing more confident in her abilities and gradually strengthening her control over powers she had once struggled to wield. Yet despite the life she managed to build, the loss of her family remained with her. She had no way of knowing whether they were safe, whether they had escaped through another passage, or whether she would ever see them again.

Yet she had over time accepted her fate, and fought hard to not let all they had done go to waste, to be in vain. But then, without warning, her past caught up with her.

An agent of the Infinite Dragonflight had somehow found her and, for reasons she could not immediately understand, attempted to drag her backward through the flow of time. Sandria's magic reacted instinctively, fighting against the temporal pull even as she was carried helplessly through the years. But rather than preventing the displacement, her resistance caused something neither she nor her captor had anticipated.

She was thrown back into her own past, several years before she had ever stepped through the portal. Yet instead of arriving as a separate presence in that time, she found herself occupying the place of her younger self, their memories merging into one.

The experience was profoundly disorienting. It was almost like waking suddenly from an unusually vivid dream, certain that what she remembered had happened, yet unable to bring every detail into focus. Memories of years she had already lived became tangled with those belonging to her younger self. Some were clear, others little more than fleeting impressions, while certain details seemed to slip away whenever she tried to grasp them. It would take time for the confusion to settle, for scattered recollections to find their places, and for Sandria to begin making sense of the two overlapping histories now occupying her mind.

The Infinite responsible for bringing her back, however, had made a grave miscalculation. The young drake before them was no longer quite the same Sandria they had expected. Though the years she had lived belonged to a future that had yet to occur, the experience and strength she had gained through them remained. Even through her disorientation, she possessed far greater control over her abilities than she had at this point in her original life. Once she recovered enough to defend herself, she was able to overwhelm her would-be captor.

But defeating the Infinite did little to explain what had happened to her. Her family was there. Saulstrasz, Callahan, and Ebbyion were all still part of a life she remembered leaving behind, unaware that anything unusual had occurred. To them, nothing had changed. Sandria had not vanished through a portal, had not spent years alone in another time, and had not returned from a future that no longer existed. Those events remained real only to her, and she chose to keep them that way.

Sandria never spoke of the years she had experienced, nor of the strange circumstances that had returned her to her family. Instead, she quietly resumed her place among them, taking time to sort through the conflicting memories now occupying her mind. As the initial confusion faded, pieces of her former life gradually fell into place. She remembered people, places, and events that had yet to occur, along with dangers she knew might still lie ahead.  For a time, this knowledge offered her a peculiar advantage. She had already lived through years that everyone around her had yet to experience, and she could use what she remembered to better prepare herself and those she cared for.

Yet time was never quite so simple.

Every choice she made carried the possibility of changing what came next, and even the smallest differences could grow into consequences she had never foreseen. What Sandria did not yet realize was that, by trying to prepare her family for a future only she remembered, she would help set them upon an entirely different path.

<h3>A Diverted Plan</h3>For a time, Sandria had believed the knowledge of her former life might prove a blessing. She remembered dangers that had yet to come, events that might be avoided, and the circumstances that had eventually torn her away from everyone she cared for. Though she never spoke of what she knew, she hoped that by preparing herself and those around her, she might spare them some of the hardships she remembered.

Of everyone in her family, it was Ebbyion who concerned her most.

Her younger brother had suffered terribly at the hands of the Infinite Dragonflight, and though his physical wounds had begun to mend, the scars left upon his mind were far more difficult to soothe. He feared the very power he had been born with, knowing what his captors had intended to make of him. Sandria had always been his protector, the one he turned to when nightmares and whispers threatened to overwhelm him, but she knew she could not always be there to shield him from the world.

With the knowledge and experience she now possessed, Sandria believed she could help him become stronger, more confident, and perhaps better prepared for whatever the future might hold.

Despite the concerns of those around them, she began encouraging Ebbyion to explore his draconic abilities, teaching him how to push beyond the enchantment of the amulet that kept his true nature concealed. It was something she had spent years learning to accomplish herself, and while the process was difficult for her brother, his efforts gradually began to bear fruit.

As a Black dragon, Ebbyion held dominion over the earth, able to shape and manipulate it through his will. Combined with Sandria's command over life and time, their abilities complemented one another remarkably well. For a while, it seemed her efforts were helping. Ebbyion was learning to wield the very power he had spent so long fearing, and Sandria hoped that, in time, he might come to see it as something other than a curse.

Unfortunately, neither of them understood just how fragile the enchantment concealing him truly was. The amulets had never been designed with Black dragons in mind, for none had remained in their twisted world when the enchantments were first created. Nor had their makers anticipated that someone might attempt to force such powerful magic through the suppression they imposed.

One quiet evening, while Ebbyion was practicing his ability to manipulate the earth, he pushed his power further than the enchantment could withstand.

His amulet cracked.

The magic that had kept his draconic nature suppressed suddenly failed, and the power he had been struggling to control surged outward all at once in a wild outburst. Without the enchantment to conceal him, his very presence became a beacon, unmistakable in a world where Black dragons were thought to no longer exist.

This surge of power was strong enough to be felt by most people, though to Sandria it was felt the strongest. She didn't know the cause but she knew who it was from, no one else in their time had the kind of magic her brother could wield. If she could feel it, she knew others could to and what kind of risk that posed for them. She quickly rushed forward to find him and discover what had happened... though her biggest fear she would find had already unfolded. 

It was not long before the Infinite found him.

They had been searching for the young Black dragon they had once intended to twist into another Deathwing, and the sudden release of his power had led them straight to him. Rather than immediately seizing their prize, they tormented him with reminders of what they believed he could become. They pointed to the corrupted earth and elementals surrounding them, blaming him for the devastation and insisting that such destruction was the inevitable result of his power.

For Ebbyion, who had spent so much of his young life terrified of that very possibility, their words struck deeply. He could scarcely bring himself to move, frozen by the fear that everything they had told him might be true.

Sandria found him surrounded by his tormentors, with the pendant that had once concealed him laying broken on the ground. Seeing her brother helpless before the very creatures who had nearly destroyed him, she acted without hesitation. She tore the enchanted amulet from her own neck, deliberately exposing her draconic nature while yelling out to them, drawing the Infinite's attention away from Ebbyion.

For the first time, her full power was no longer restrained by the pendant's enchantment.

Drawing upon her Bronze heritage, Sandria manipulated the flow of time around them, slowing it to a standstill and countering the temporal magic of their pursuers. She seized the opportunity to reach her brother, placing her own amulet around his neck to conceal him once more before grabbing his from the ground and fleeing with him.

But she could not keep him at her side while attempting to undo the damage to his pendant. As much control of her natural magic she had, she could not divide it to both reverse the effects that led to the pendants damage and keep them concealed in time. Once she had hidden him somewhere safe, Sandria continued on alone, time around her resumed while she put her focus on trying to return the fractured amulet to a moment before it had been broken.

She eventually returned to Ebbyion, exhausted and battered, but successful. His pendant had been restored, and she wore it around her own neck while her brother remained protected by hers. She succeeded in its repairs as she warded off and evaded her pursuers, then slipped it on and used her magic to pause time again to finally slip away from their endless pursuit.

They had escaped, but the encounter had taken a terrible toll.

Rather than reassuring Ebbyion that he could learn to control his abilities, the incident had reinforced everything he feared about himself. He wanted nothing more to do with his draconic powers, terrified that using them would inevitably lead him down the path the Infinite had intended. Sandria could hardly blame him after what he had endured, though she could not escape the knowledge that it had been her encouragement that led him to push his abilities in the first place.

Worse still, the consequences reached far beyond her brother's renewed fears. Their concealment had been compromised. The Infinite now knew that both the young Black dragon and the Red-Bronze hybrid they had once created were still alive, and they would not abandon their pursuit so easily.

The family could no longer remain where they were.

<h3>A Diverted Repeat</h3>With their enemies becoming increasingly active, the remaining uncorrupted Bronze dragons had already been working toward a desperate solution: opening passages into other timelines, sending those they could save into a past untouched by the devastation of their own world. It was a plan Sandria remembered all too well, though the circumstances surrounding it were now vastly different.

There would be no careful preparations this time, no orderly departure, and no opportunity to ensure everything was ready before they left. Saulstrasz hurried the small family to the Nexus, the ancient fortress in Coldarra that had once served as the home of the Blue Dragonflight. There, with the assistance of the few Bronze dragons who still resisted the Infinite's corruption, they hoped the powerful magic of the Nexus would conceal them long enough to open a passage into another time.

For Sandria, the familiarity of the plan was deeply unsettling. She remembered what had happened when she had stepped through such a portal before, how a misunderstanding had severed her connection to her family and left her stranded for years. Yet everything leading to this moment had changed. The danger was immediate, their pursuers were closing in, and she no longer had the luxury of believing she knew what would happen next.

The fragile hope of an orderly departure vanished as their pursuers descended upon the family. Saulstrasz threw himself into their path, desperately trying to buy the others enough time to escape. Before their horrified eyes, the Red dragon was mortally wounded, his neck torn open as he gave his life to hold the Infinite back.

For a moment, Sandria and Ebbyion could do little more than stare.

It was Callahan who broke them from their shock. As the portal finally opened, he seized the siblings and forced them toward it. There was no time for farewells, no chance to turn back for Saulstrasz, and no opportunity to question where the passage might lead. Callahan pushed them through, sending his children away from the danger even as he remained behind.

Sandria and Ebbyion emerged together on the other side, only to watch in horror as the portal closed behind them.

They were alive, but alone...

The passage had carried them into an unfamiliar timeline, somewhere in Azeroth's past, long before many of the events they knew would come to pass. They had escaped the Infinite's immediate grasp, but the cost had been devastating. Saulstrasz was gone, Callahan had been left behind, and there was no way of knowing whether they would ever see their home or the rest of their family again.

Sandria had once lived through a similar separation, years of solitude that only she could remember. She had believed those memories might help her prevent such a fate from happening again. Instead, she found herself facing something entirely different.

The future she remembered had been altered beyond recognition. The circumstances of their escape, the loss of Saulstrasz, the presence of her brother beside her, and even the point in time at which they had arrived were no longer the same. Whatever knowledge she had carried from her former life could offer little guidance now.

Yet despite everything they had lost, one thing was different.

This time, she was not alone. Ebbyion was beside her, frightened and grieving, but alive. Whatever uncertainties awaited them in this unfamiliar world, Sandria knew she would face them with her brother, just as she had promised him so long ago.

She would be his strength, his shield, and his wings.

<H3>Another Time, another Place.</h3>The passage had brought them to the Nexus, though not the one they had known. The ancient fortress still stood within the frozen reaches of Coldarra, but the world surrounding it was vastly different from the dying timeway they had escaped. Here, the Blue Dragonflight still held its ancient home, and the lands beyond had not yet suffered the terrible fate that had consumed their own world.

Yet for Sandria and Ebbyion, such differences meant little in those first moments.

They had escaped with their lives, but the events leading to their arrival had left them devastated. Saulstrasz may be dead, Callahan had been left behind, and the portal that had carried them to safety had closed without any means of returning. They were stranded in an unfamiliar past, with no way of knowing what had become of the family they had lost.

For a time, the two could do little more than remain together, overwhelmed by grief and uncertainty. Their arrival, however, had not gone entirely unnoticed.

The disturbance their passage had caused within the timeways had drawn the attention of a rather peculiar little gnome, or so she appeared. In truth, she was Chronormu, better known as Chromie, a member of the Bronze Dragonflight who had come to investigate the unexpected disruption. What she found was hardly what she had anticipated, even for someone like her.

Two frightened strangers stood before her, clearly shaken by whatever ordeal had brought them there. Sandria, still on edge from the events of their escape, immediately positioned herself protectively before her brother, unwilling to risk allowing another unfamiliar dragon near him. Ebbyion remained close behind her, withdrawn and silent, struggling to comprehend everything that had happened.

Chromie was quick to assure them that they were in no danger from her, though she could sense the lingering traces of temporal magic surrounding them and calmly explained why she was there. Whatever had brought these two into her timeline was certainly worth investigating, but their condition suggested that there were more immediate concerns to address.

The sudden appearance of the siblings had also been noticed by the Blue Dragonflight, whose members had alerted Kalecgos to the unexpected visitors within their territory. With both a Bronze and a Blue dragon now taking an interest in them, Sandria's apprehension only grew. She had spent much of her life hiding what she truly was, and the thought of being surrounded by dragons she knew nothing about did little to ease her fears.

Still, Chromie's reassurances gradually helped settle the situation. Neither she nor Kalecgos appeared intent on harming them, and it was becoming increasingly clear that the siblings were in no condition to fend for themselves.

Reluctantly, Sandria accepted their offer of shelter when it was given to them. The two were brought into the Nexus and given a room where they could rest while their unexpected arrival was investigated. For the first time since fleeing their home, they were somewhere relatively safe, though safety alone could do little to ease the grief weighing upon them. Once they had been given some time to recover, Chromie and Kalecgos returned to speak with them.

Ebbyion remained largely silent throughout the conversation, unable or unwilling to speak of what he had endured. Sandria, despite struggling with her own grief, took it upon herself to explain their circumstances. She told them of the twisted timeway they had come from, of the Infinite Dragonflight's hold over their world, and of the desperate attempt to escape that had cost Saulstrasz his life and separated them from Callahan.

With Sandria's permission, Chromie used her own command over time to examine the events surrounding their departure, witnessing fragments of their recent history as though watching the past unfold before her. For the Bronze dragon, such things were hardly beyond comprehension. Timeways, altered histories, and journeys through the past were familiar territory, even if the circumstances surrounding these particular travelers were troubling.

What Chromie made of everything she witnessed, however, was not something she readily shared.

Later, after the siblings had been allowed further rest, Sandria sought the Bronze dragon out alone. There were matters she wished to discuss away from her brother, concerns that had been weighing upon her since their arrival. She pleaded with Chromie not to reveal her existence to the rest of the Bronze Dragonflight, at least not yet. Nor did she wish to be handed over to the Red Dragonflight simply because of her heritage. She had spent much of her life being hidden, passed between guardians for her own protection, and the thought of suddenly being claimed by dragons she had never known was deeply unsettling.

But her greatest concern was Ebbyion.

He was a Black dragon, one free of the corruption that plagued his flight, a fact that carried considerable weight in this unfamiliar world. Sandria knew little of what awaited them here, but she understood enough of the history surrounding his flight to fear what might happen if his identity became widely known. Her brother had already suffered terribly because of what others believed he could become, and she refused to see him subjected to such treatment again. She asked that his true nature remain a secret, just as hers would.

The enchanted pendants they wore would help with that. So long as their magic remained intact, the siblings would appear to be ordinary mortals, their draconic natures concealed even from other dragons. Chromie agreed to keep their identities closely guarded.  Whether she did so simply out of sympathy for their circumstances, or because she knew more about Sandria than she cared to admit, was difficult to say. The little Bronze had an uncanny way of seeming to know things she had no reason to know, and Sandria could never be entirely certain just how much of her history Chromie had seen.

Perhaps she knew only what Sandria had allowed her to witness. Or perhaps she knew far more. Whatever the case, Chromie respected her wishes, and the siblings were permitted to remain within the Nexus for several days while they recovered and considered what to do next.

It was during this time that Kalecgos returned with an unexpected proposal. After speaking with Chromie and considering the siblings' circumstances, he had decided to offer them some assistance in finding their place within this unfamiliar timeline. He introduced them to Tobi, a young Blue drake whom he wished to assign as their guide and companion.

Tobi would help the siblings familiarize themselves with the world around them, explain the differences between this timeline and the one they had known, and assist them in navigating lands and cultures that might otherwise prove difficult to understand. As a mage, he was also capable of opening portals to distant locations, allowing them to travel without undertaking every journey on foot.

There was, however, another purpose to his assignment as Kalecgos wished for Tobi to keep an eye on the unusual newcomers and report back should anything concerning arise. Though he was willing to help them, the Blue dragon was not entirely prepared to leave two mysterious travelers from another timeway to wander Azeroth without some measure of oversight.

Sandria was understandably hesitant, after spending so much of her life hiding from others, the idea of having someone assigned to accompany them was hardly comforting. She was uncertain how much she could trust Tobi, or whether his presence might eventually threaten the secrecy she had asked Chromie to protect.

Yet she could not deny the practicality of the arrangement. The world beyond the Nexus was unfamiliar, and the memories she carried from her former life could no longer be relied upon to guide her. Too much had changed, and even the point in time at which they had arrived was different from the one she remembered. Having someone who understood this world's history, its people, and its dangers would be invaluable.

More than that, perhaps having another friendly face nearby would not be such a terrible thing.

After some consideration, Sandria agreed.

With Tobi accompanying them, the siblings would eventually leave the relative safety of the Nexus and begin exploring the frozen lands of Northrend. What awaited them beyond its ancient walls remained uncertain, but for the first time since their arrival, they had something resembling a direction in which to move.

Their old lives were gone, and the future before them was one neither could predict.

But they were together, and perhaps, in time, they might find a place to call home.



[PERSONALITY]
<h3>Temperament</h3>Sandria is a headstrong and determined individual, possessing a stubborn streak that has followed her since her earliest years. Once she sets her mind to something, it can be difficult to convince her otherwise, especially when she believes her actions are justified. She is not easily intimidated and rarely backs down from a challenge, preferring to face difficulties head-on rather than wait for someone else to resolve them. Though generally patient and levelheaded, her resolve can sometimes get the better of her, leading her to act before fully considering the consequences.

Despite her strong will, Sandria is not an inherently confrontational individual. She generally prefers to approach others with understanding and reason, offering kindness where she can and standing firm where she feels it necessary. She can be surprisingly warm and good-humored when comfortable, though her patience has its limits, particularly when those she cares for are threatened.

When challenged on something she feels strongly about, Sandria can become rather argumentative, especially if she believes someone is being unreasonable or refusing to consider another perspective. Her stubbornness often makes her reluctant to concede a point, and she has been known to continue an argument well beyond the point where others might have given up. While she does not deliberately seek conflict, her determination to make herself understood can sometimes make her come across as more forceful than she intends.

On rare occasions, when an argument becomes particularly frustrating or she finds herself needing a moment to collect her thoughts, Sandria has been known to make rather unconventional use of her Bronze heritage. By briefly suspending the flow of time around herself, she can take a moment to vent her frustrations, pace about, or simply consider her next response before allowing time to resume. To those unaware of what she has done, the pause passes unnoticed, though anyone watching may find her suddenly standing somewhere other than where she had been moments before. She is careful to avoid such displays around those who are unaware of her true nature, and generally reserves the practice for moments when she feels she genuinely needs a moment to herself.

Years of hardship have taught her when to exercise restraint, though they have done little to diminish her stubborn nature. She has learned to weigh her decisions more carefully, often showing a maturity and composure beyond what others might expect of someone her apparent age. Yet even with that experience, she remains prone to taking matters into her own hands when she feels something must be done.


 
<h3>Prejudices</h3>Sandria is not inclined to condemn someone simply for their race, their dragonflight, or the reputation that follows them. Living as a hybrid, and later becoming sister to a Black dragon who had already been judged for what others feared he might become, has given her little patience for such assumptions. She is far more concerned with what someone chooses to do than with what they were born as.

That does not mean she is free of prejudice herself. Her experiences have left her with a deep distrust of the Infinite Dragonflight and a particular wariness of those who believe authority gives them the right to decide another person's fate. Even the uncorrupted Bronze Dragonflight initially unsettles her. In a life no one else remembers, their insistence that she return to her own doomed timeway left a wound she has never spoken of. The kindness she encounters in this new history challenges that expectation, but accepting that these dragons may be different takes time. She is equally wary of being claimed by the Red Dragonflight merely because she carries their blood.

Her mistrust is strongest when someone treats Ebbyion as a threat or a curiosity rather than a person. She knows the danger associated with his heritage, but refuses to accept that it determines who he must become.

<h3>Moral Values</h3>Much of Sandria's sense of right and wrong comes from the life she shared with Callahan. His example as a paladin taught her that strength should be used in service of others, whether through healing, protection, or the quieter work of helping someone endure another day. She believes that life has value and that people deserve some say in the course of their own lives, beliefs shaped as much by the circumstances of her creation as by anything she was taught.

She does, however, distinguish between what is lawful and what is right. Sandria will usually look for an honest or peaceful solution first, but she is not above bending a rule, concealing the truth, or using her abilities discreetly when she believes there is no reasonable alternative. Necessity is not an excuse she takes lightly, nor does she regard cruelty or retaliation as justified simply because she has been wronged. Her choices are often guided by what will keep people alive rather than by an ideal of perfect conduct.

Her strongest convictions can also become her greatest failing. Sandria takes promises seriously, especially the one she made to be Ebbyion's strength, shield, and wings. Yet in trying to protect him from every danger, she sometimes makes decisions on his behalf without realizing how little room that leaves him to grow. Learning that care and control are not always as different as she would like to believe is an uncomfortable lesson she has only begun to face.

<h3>Ambitions</h3>Sandria has little interest in rank, recognition, or claiming a place of importance among the dragonflights. For much of her life, simply surviving without being discovered was ambition enough. Having escaped that world, she hopes for something more ordinary, though no less important to her: a home where she and Ebbyion can live without constantly fearing that their true natures will be used against them.

She wishes to continue developing her skills as a paladin, a healer, and a smith, making use of the knowledge Callahan gave her rather than allowing those years to be defined only by hardship. She is also determined to better understand her own mixture of Red and Bronze magic, particularly the abilities that still emerge beyond her conscious control. What she wants is command over those gifts, not the power or status others might associate with them.

Above all, she wants Ebbyion to have a future that belongs to him. She hopes he will one day feel safe enough to trust his own judgment and see his heritage as something other than a curse, even if helping him reach that point means learning to step back herself. The fate of those left behind, particularly Callahan and the mother she never truly knew, remains an unanswered ache rather than a plan she knows how to pursue.

<h3>Likes</h3>Sandria finds comfort in work that leaves something tangible behind. The rhythm of a forge, the satisfaction of mending a damaged tool, and the sight of a stubborn crop finally taking root all remind her of the life Callahan helped build for her. She enjoys being useful without having to explain herself, especially when a small act of effort or healing makes someone else's day a little easier.

She also enjoys learning, particularly through books and by watching others practice skills she has not yet mastered. Having grown up with few opportunities for formal instruction, her curiosity has often been satisfied through observation and trial and error. Music holds a more personal place in her life. She loves to sing, drawn in part by the faint memory of a voice she believes belonged to her mother, heard long ago through the shell of her egg.

More than anything, she values the quieter moments in which Ebbyion can relax and simply be her brother, rather than someone she must constantly defend.

<h3>Dislikes</h3>Few things anger Sandria more than needless cruelty, particularly when it is inflicted upon someone who has little power to resist. She has no tolerance for the Infinite Dragonflight's experiments or for anyone who treats living beings as tools to be altered, possessed, or discarded. Being told that a person's nature or future has already been decided for them is especially likely to provoke her temper.

She dislikes being pressured to surrender her independence, whether by someone demanding obedience or by a dragonflight presuming a claim over her because of her ancestry. She is similarly frustrated by hypocrisy, especially when rules presented as righteous are used to deny help to those who need it. Though she understands the value of caution and secrecy, she resents the necessity of hiding who she is and the suspicion that so often follows those who are different.

On a more personal level, she has little patience for feeling helpless or being forced to watch while someone she loves suffers. That frustration is one reason she can be so stubborn about intervening, even when restraint might serve her better.

<h3>Fears</h3>Sandria's deepest fear is losing what remains of her family. She has already lived through years of separation that no one else remembers, followed by an escape that cost Saulstrasz his life and left Callahan beyond her reach. The thought of being left alone again troubles her more than she likes to admit, but the possibility of failing to protect Ebbyion frightens her more.

She fears what might happen if their identities are exposed to those who would exploit them, or if another failure of the enchanted pendants leaves them defenseless. For Ebbyion in particular, she worries that the cruelty of the Infinite has convinced him he can never be anything other than what they intended. She is painfully aware that the danger he faced after his pendant broke began with a lesson she had encouraged.

There is also a quieter uncertainty she carries regarding her own power over time. Sandria once believed that remembering the future might allow her to spare her family its worst hardships. Instead, her attempts to change what she knew helped bring about consequences she could never have predicted. She fears making that mistake again, and fears, too, that her determination to keep Ebbyion safe may become another burden he must eventually overcome.

<h3>Habits</h3>Sandria has a tendency to place herself between Ebbyion and anyone or anything she considers a possible threat, often without realizing she has done it. She is equally inclined to answer difficult questions on his behalf when strangers are involved, a habit born of years spent protecting his secrets. It can take conscious effort for her to let him speak or choose for himself.

She keeps her enchanted pendant close and out of sight beneath her clothing, just as she tends to keep her old libram within reach. When troubled, she often turns to something practical, finding that working with her hands gives her room to think. At quieter times she may hum or sing softly, sometimes without noticing she has begun.

Her Bronze magic occasionally becomes part of these habits. When especially frustrated, she has been known to slip briefly outside the normal flow of time to collect her thoughts, pace off her irritation, or simply escape an overwhelming moment. She is careful about doing so where it might expose her true nature, though those close to her may have learned to recognize the occasional unexplained change in where she is standing.

At first, Sandria was wary of having someone appointed to observe them, especially knowing that Tobi was expected to report back to Kalecgos.

Tobi came to understand how much the siblings valued their privacy and began withholding details that he did not believe Kalecgos needed to know. In time, Kalecgos relieved him of his reporting duty, asking only that he continue looking out for their safety. For Sandria, whose first instinct is to shoulder every responsibility herself, Tobi's friendship has become particularly important. He is one of the few people she trusts to stay with Ebbyion when she cannot, and someone she has learned to count upon for more than his skill with magic.
