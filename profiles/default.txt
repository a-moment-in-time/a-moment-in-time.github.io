   * To make a new section header: 
<h1>H1 Title</h1> - Largest size, underlined. Ideal for title sections.
<h2>H2 Title</h2> - 2nd largest, idea for key headers.
<h3>H3 Title</h3> - Mid range text, all caps, underlined. Ideal for chapter/section names.
<h4>H4 Title</h4>  - Smaller sub sections. 
<h5>H5 Title</h5> -  Smallest section, mainly just to point something out.
