[NOTES]
Eldest of three born to Priestess Noraisha and Archdruid Mathordor Stormweaver
She is viewed as a deserter to her families cause by her father, due to forgoing the path her family desired of her (a priest) and chose to become a huntress instead. This was done in protest to her fathers abusive treatment to her younger brother causing him to flee from home, and vanishing into the world.
Through her respect and devotion to nature, she has earned the trust and favor of many forest spirits (Spirit Beast's) that come to her aid when called.
She's a firm believer that one should not judge individuals based on the actions of others, rather horde or alliance. Just because there's a few or a group of bad apples, don't mean they all are that way.
She is very much neutral when it comes to the alliance/horde conflict. To the point she hated Varian, blaming him for being blind and encouraging the continued conflict every time peace had a chance to happen.
She has an half orc son whom she gave up willingly per agreement to aid an old Orc friend in acquiring a heir to his bloodline.
