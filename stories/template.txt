A MOMENT IN TIME — STORY TEXT TEMPLATE
======================================
Keep the bracketed field names as written. The text immediately BELOW each
field belongs to it, up until the next bracketed field. Blank optional
fields are hidden on the story page.

Authors can type their stories normally, leaving a blank line between
paragraphs. Simple HTML such as <b>bold</b>, <i>italics</i>, and
<a href="https://example.com">links</a> is supported, just like the bios.
Only paste HTML you trust into a story file.

The story page reads: stories/index.html?story=your-filename
Example: stories/index.html?story=example-story&chapter=2
The filename is lowercase letters, numbers, underscores or hyphens;
no .txt in the URL.

CHAPTERS ARE AUTOMATIC — NO LIMIT:
Add [CHAPTER_3_TITLE] and [CHAPTER_3] for chapter 3, then chapter 4, etc.
There is NO need to update the HTML or JavaScript. A chapter shows up only
when its [CHAPTER_NUMBER] text is not blank. Chapter titles are optional.
To preserve existing links, keep published chapter numbers stable.
To add another chapter, copy its TITLE, NOTE, CHAPTER and END_NOTE fields
and change the number; only the [CHAPTER_N] body is required.

OPTIONAL AUTHOR NOTES:
[NOTE] appears below the story information, before chapter navigation.
[CHAPTER_1_NOTE] appears at the start of chapter 1 (after its title).
[CHAPTER_1_END_NOTE] appears at the end of chapter 1, before navigation.
Replace the number for each chapter, e.g. [CHAPTER_17_END_NOTE].
All notes are optional. Blank ones are completely hidden. Use them for
writing updates, hiatus notices, chapter context, or author comments.

RATING SUGGESTIONS (type whichever applies):
General Audiences / Teen / Mature / Explicit / Not Rated
Warnings are free text, not a required choice from a fixed list.
An empty warning field means no warnings were supplied — it is NOT an
assurance that the work contains no potentially upsetting content.

Do not place text inside a [FIELD] as a prompt unless you want it
published! Replace the example filler below with your actual content.
