[NOTE]
<b>Story note:</b>  This is a ‘What If’ concept planning and notes that has been made cannon. It started after Ebbyion was created as a character, (he was called Ebyion/Eben back then, I took the time to correct the spelling throughout this to avoid confusion) back during the Pandaria expansion (2013). 

Ebben first came into existence as a thought from another friend Jordan, who loved Wrathion (and the one who developed our story lines around the infinite flight) came up with the idea of "What if the Infinite had captured Wrathion from the timeline?" that ended up evolving into "What if the Infinite stole his egg from another timeline with the plans to twist that version of him into another Deathwing to use as a weapon against the time-ways?"

Thus Ebben’s original concept came into being, and he was added to Sandria’s story sense they both would be dealing with the infinite flight. The original plans for Ebben after he was first made were that he would remain out of the picture, as to not affect the story we already had for Mae. (never made it through the portal with her) But later on, he would be revealed as a large corrupt dragon, with the Infinite having succeeded in turning him into another Deathwing under their control. It was planed (though we never did RP it as it was still a future concept) to result in a long battle with the Bronze flight that ended with the only 'fix' to Ebben state, other than killing him, being an attempt to time warp him back far enough before the corruption took hold. It would undo the effects to his body, but not fully his mind or memories of what he had become, leaving him heavily traumatized.

This ‘what if’ however came about as thoughts of ‘What if Ebben did make it through with Sandria?’ that furthered into ‘What if Ebben met ‘himself?’ This is what started this story and turned it into far more then just ‘a wrathion meeting wrathion’. Being made cannon had Sandria’s whole story re-written to include him, her alternate time experience so we didn’t have to fully erase everything, and how they ended up being together in the world.
------------------------
Things to note with this ‘what if’ concept:

* This is Ebben’s first written concept/storyboard as a character. Thus not everything about him was flushed out at this time, and some ideas for him developed as a result of this as it was drafted (like his damaged arm became a thing mid way, though it doesn’t reflect the exact damage as its been developed). 
* This remained a ‘what if’ story for a time until we later on made it cannon, so as of this, Sandria’s history had not yet been altered with her being pulled back to her past to relive things and instead is viewed as if Ebben had made it through with her originally.
* Tobi didn’t exist as a character at this time, so he is nowhere to be found. He only came into being after this was made cannon and Sandria got a detailed re-write.
-------

When this is adapted to a proper story form, things will need to be altered in places to properly reflect Ebben and Sandria’s current history’s and their developments as characters. Plus to keep things consistent rather than ‘made up’ as things went along. Tobi would also be traveling with them, though maybe not always by their side for every scene as hes still reporting to Kelec or being asked to do things, like ‘hey can you go back to __ and grab this for us?” kind of stuff. Or doing his own thing as the siblings go about their day and doing some work for the people.

[CHAPTER_1_TITLE]

Summery of how they got there.

[CHAPTER_1]
The Cataclysm started, Deathwing emerged and started his reign of terror across the world. As time went on and heros began to confront the threat, word of a ‘black prince’ and his agents under him meticulously culling members of the black flight would start spreading around. Reaching Maetel and Ebben as they try to lend aid where they can in a world that has drastically changed.

The concern that Ebben could be discovered and be on the hit-list started to grow as word grew, nearly all the black dragons were gone and the few remaining were being hunted down as they went into hiding. This culling was very efficient.

Maetel would dive into memories, and recall a place they may be able to escape to, told from a Pandarin they had known in their home timeway so Maetel makes the move to fly Ebben out to Pandaria, remembering the descriptions of where it was in the world well before anyone else ever arrived, managing to push past the mist that guarded the island to find refuge.

They would be there for a time, eventually be discovered, and welcomed as visitors to a small village where they would settle in, answer questions on how they arrived and found the place. They would settle in, and explore these lands finding many places that they both find to be peaceful, even ideas that these lands could end up being their home. Ebben is very into the Lorewalkers and what they are willing to share. Mae would love the Tillers, and openly use her dragon magic to help the farmlands grow their crops.

Then the events of the Pandaria expansion start, with the ships holding the ‘white pawn’ Anduin crashing into the island still covered in mist, with the horde, doing much the same with their air ships.

This brings the alliance and horde to Pandaira and the conflict resumes much to Maetel and Ebben’s annoyance… They continue to try and stay out of the conflicts.
