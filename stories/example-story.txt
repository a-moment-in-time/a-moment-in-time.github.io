[CHAPTER_1_TITLE]
The Sealed Ledger

[CHAPTER_1_NOTE]
An author note can appear before a chapter. This one is here to show the layout.

[CHAPTER_1]
The archivist had put up a sign on the library door. It read: <i>PLEASE DO NOT DISTURB THE CHRONICLES.</i>

Naturally, the very next visitor knocked three times and asked if the chronicles could be disturbed just a little.

"Only if you promise to put them back in chronological order," the archivist replied.

[CHAPTER_1_END_NOTE]
Thanks for reading Chapter 1! End notes show beneath the story text.

[CHAPTER_2_TITLE]
A Page Out of Order

[CHAPTER_2_NOTE]

[CHAPTER_2]
The second chapter appeared with hardly any effort. No one had to make a new webpage, and nobody was forced to count how many chapters the library might hold. Best of all no added HTML or coding was needed. Just simple brackets that helped divide the chapter into another. 

"Can I skip directly to the next chapter?" the visitor asked.

"Of course. You can even share the <b>chapter's own URL</b>," the archivist said, looking very pleased with the new system.

[CHAPTER_2_END_NOTE]

[CHAPTER_3_TITLE]
The End of the Test

[CHAPTER_3_NOTE]

[CHAPTER_3]
One final page slid neatly into the ledger. The previous and next buttons knew exactly where it belonged.

And with that, the archivist declared the test complete — though everyone knew better than to call a website <i>final</i>.
