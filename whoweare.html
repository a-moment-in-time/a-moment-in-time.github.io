Who we Are

Welcome to our little respite that will offer you a touch into who we as the players of these characters are. Most of us have way more characters and/or in-game alts than we can keep tabs on. If we included them all this page listing would end up being a few pages long, so we all settled on the top 5-6 'main' characters we most commonly include in current Role Plays. For example, William has over three FULL game realms of characters he role-plays as, how he keeps tabs on them all is somehow locked in his mind.

Outside our own characters, we will also Role Play out NPC's, both made up and official characters. Such roles are often shared with two or more of us taking up the character to help avoid scenarios of us Role Playing to ourselves (though there are times it can't be helped), or if one has a better idea for how a situation may better play out. For example, both Will and Sami are the ones most often taking up roleplaying as Anduin Wrynn.

Eric - The Dungeon Master

Pronouns: He/Him
Age Range: Early 50's
Primary Game Master of the group. Also known as 'Lil Man', due to being short in stature and baby-faced (when he shaves his beard off) to where more than once he has been mistaken as the ‘son’ to his adult daughter, plus all the stories from his days in the Navy (retired). A No-Nonsense type, if something happens or a disagreement can't be worked out he is often the one to come in and settle matters, going on facts rather than hearsay.

    • Other Family & Friends who join us from time to time.

    Outside the core members of our group, other friends and family have joined us on our ventures and contributed their own characters and stories to the mix. While their characters may not be listed here on this site, they may appear in stories we share. But also note if there is any privacy concerns, like if they didn't want their character showcased out in a bigger public world, or if a story contained more personal content... then those stories are either kept private, or with permission have their characters names, and some details replaced with another's. We strive to assure everyone is comfortable and respect boundaries while we explore these worlds and the characters we have in them.
